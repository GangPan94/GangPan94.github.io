Renaissance Accounting Odyssey (Florence, c. 1482)

Level 1: Single-Entry Bookkeeping

Welcome, Master Banker! Patrons will arrive at your counter one by one. Search the historical single-entry ledger scroll to match deposit notes with items in the giant vault. Keep patron sanity high and protect the bank's reputation!

📜 Rules of Renaissance Banking

Welcome to 15th Century Florence, Banker!


Goal: Serve 3 bank patrons by fulfilling valid claims or rejecting fraudulent claims before their Sanity/Patience drops to zero!


Step-by-Step Workflow:

  1. Click Select Patron on an arriving patron at the counter.
  2. Search the Bookkeeping Ledger and click the entry/account matching the patron to highlight it as audit proof.
  3. For a Valid Claim: Click the matching vault item/drawer, then click 💰 Hand Vault Item.
  4. For a False Claim: Click 🚫 Reject False Claim to catch the fraud!

⚠️ Warning: If patron sanity reaches 0s, their eyes will turn shiny red and shoot lasers at the bank, destroying 2 Bank Reputation HP every second!
