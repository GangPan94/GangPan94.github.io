FIFO Queue Discipline

  • • Discipline: Strict FIFO Arrival Order
  • • Variance in Item Holding Time: Low
  • • Risk of Item Spoilage/Obsolescence: Minimized
  • • Physical Flow Match: Perishable Goods (Food, Pharma)

LIFO Stack Discipline

  • • Discipline: Strict LIFO (Push / Pop Stack)
  • • Variance in Item Holding Time: High (Base stays forever)
  • • Risk of Item Spoilage/Obsolescence: High for deep base
  • • Physical Flow Match: Bulk piles (Coal, Gravel, Ore)
