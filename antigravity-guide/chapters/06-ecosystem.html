Appendix A — Academic AI Setups & Ecosystem

This appendix provides a curated directory of open-source setups, skill libraries, and workflow repositories developed by the academic community for economics, finance, and empirical research. You can explore or fork any of these repositories to add specialized capabilities to your project workspace.

A.1 Empirical Economics & Data Workflows

  • Barrios88/barrios-skills (John Barrios, Washington University in St. Louis): Over 70 modular skills and MCP servers for empirical economics, Wharton Research Data Services (WRDS) access, SEC EDGAR filing extraction, financial NLP, and John Cochrane-inspired writing rules.
  • A Causal Affair (Paul Goldsmith-Pinkham, Yale School of Management): Practical essays and video series on agentic AI workflows, out-of-core data pipelines with DuckDB and Polars, and unstructured-to-structured database building.
  • SepineTam/stata-mcp (SepineTam): A Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that enables AI agents to execute Stata .do files securely with command safeguards and memory monitoring.
  • meleantonio/awesome-econ-ai-stuff (Antonio Mele, USI / Swiss Finance Institute): A curated directory of AI prompts, instructions, and workflows tailored for economics and finance researchers.

A.2 Paper Writing & Peer Review

  • hugosantanna/clo-author (Hugo Sant’Anna, UAB): A paper-centric research workflow featuring adversarial worker-critic agent pairs, simulated peer review, and journal submission pipelines.
  • aspi6246/GeminiCodeTools: Structured academic reviewer personas (“The Editor”) executing sequential multi-pass audits and referee report response routing.
  • morankor/theorist-toolbox (Moran Koren, Ben-Gurion University): An AI co-mathematician framework for theoretical economics, game theory, and formal Lean 4 theorem proving.

A.3 Reproducibility & Research Standards

  • social-science-data-editors/template_README (Lars Vilhuber, Cornell / AEA Data Editor): The official compliance template for replication packages across major economics and social science journals.
  • Scaling Reproducibility Paper (Yiqing Xu, Stanford & Leo Yang Yang, HKBU): Foundational paper on template-executor architecture and automated replication auditing.

A.4 Presentations & General Academic Life

  • scunning1975/MixtapeTools (Scott Cunningham, Baylor University): Presentation design philosophy (“The Rhetoric of Decks”), Beamer themes, and adversarial empirical auditing prompts.
  • chrisblattman/claudeblattman (Chris Blattman, University of Chicago / claudeblattman.com): Workflows for non-technical academics covering grant proposals, executive assistance, and fresh-context critique.
  • karpathy/autoresearch (Andrej Karpathy): Constraint-based autonomous experimentation framework using Markdown constitutional rules (program.md).

A.5 Cross-Platform Migration Tools

  • openai/skills (migrate-to-codex) (OpenAI): Official curated migration skill to convert Claude Code or Cursor instructions and skills into native OpenAI Codex format (AGENTS.md and .codex/ configs).
