Firms accumulate output nobody has checked, and a dashboard that counts tokens rewards only the side that creates the backlog. I propose an internal accounting system in which generated output enters as an “asset,” the same entry raises a “liability” for the review owed before the firm can use that output, and clearing the review reclassifies the amount as verified content equity. The system prices the backlog, which unit counts convert into days of review; past a computable batch size, generating and checking output can cost more than a more manual process does. A firm that records generation and review together can say how much of its AI output is usable and what the rest will cost to make usable.'
