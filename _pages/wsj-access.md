# Wall Street Journal Access Options

Two ways to read the Wall Street Journal for this course — choose whichever works best for you.

## Option 1 — Free via UA Libraries (Factiva)

[Access WSJ articles through Factiva](https://libguides.uakron.edu/az/factiva){: target="_blank" rel="noopener" }

The UA Libraries subscribe to Factiva, which includes the full text of Wall Street Journal articles. This is completely free with your university login. The format is text-only — no layout, images, or interactive features — but every article is available.

## Option 2 — WSJ Student Discount (via Dr. Pan's referral)

[Get the student discount](https://r.wsj.com/PROFwc6u){: target="_blank" rel="noopener" }

If you prefer the traditional WSJ reading experience (full layout, images, apps), you can subscribe at a steep student discount through Dr. Pan's referral link. The discount is **at least 75% off** the standard subscription price, and even greater if you are a new subscriber.
